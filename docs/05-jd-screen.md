# 05. Screening a job posting

For a pasted posting (or a link you can open), answer in exactly this shape. No essays. Sample: `examples/john-smith/sample-screen.md`.

**Verdict: APPLY / SKIP / APPLY-IF (condition) / RATE-FIRST (agency)**

Then 3 to 6 bullets, only what decides it:
- Pay against their floor and the market band in `<data>/SCREEN.md`
- Fit: matching stories and bullets, and what's missing
- Level: their tier, a stretch, or below them
- Any hard gate it trips
- Strong APPLY: resume lines to lead with, and who to contact (`docs/08-outreach.md`)

## Hard gates (each decides the verdict alone)

Thresholds are in `<data>/SCREEN.md`, set in the intake.

1. **Staffing agency: RATE-FIRST.** No resume until the client's name and the rate are settled.
2. **Contract while they hold a permanent job:** must clear their hourly floor and minimum length. A short fixed-term contract needs a reason beyond the rate.
3. **Salaried floor:** below it, SKIP. Just above it, APPLY-IF the role adds something money doesn't (title, lane, a big name).
4. **No pay posted** where the law requires it: yellow flag. Get the number first.
5. **Refused titles** from the intake: SKIP.
6. **Seniority** follows the intake answer (phase 3, question 3). Default under about 5 years in the lane: skip Senior, Lead, Principal, Staff, Architect and Manager titles unless the user chose "stretch up". Never let an AI talk the user into a level they didn't pick.
7. **Years asked far above the user's** (10+ asked, 3 held): SKIP unless the user is pitching for a role on that team through outreach.
8. **Scope below their level** (queue work, no ownership): SKIP unless the money changes their life.
9. **Their deal breakers** (travel, on-call, relocation, clearance, stack).

## Market bands

Keep a short table in `<data>/SCREEN.md`: title, years, range, source, month. Build it from postings that disclose pay plus one or two salary surveys. Re-check it after 6 months. Never present a band as fact without its source and date.

## Default posture

Employed: every APPLY must beat staying (raise, title, cert or bonus on their current path), not just their current pay. Between jobs: lower the bar for "worth a conversation" and keep the floor.
