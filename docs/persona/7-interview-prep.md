# Persona 7. Interview prep

Goal: spoken answers that match the resume and the profile. Use only confirmed facts from STORIES, NUMBERS,
SKILLS, PERSONA, REFERENCES and SCREEN. Never invent a story, a number or a claim for an interview.

## One folder per company

`<data>/interviews/<company>/` (as in `../08-outreach.md`):
- `posting.md`: the posting, the stage, and who they will meet (roles) if known.
- `fit-map.md`: each main requirement, with the story or SKILLS row that answers it, or "gap".
- `prep.md`: the pitch, the story map, the careful-topic lines and their questions (below).
- `round-N-review.md`: the notes after each round.

## The story map

Pick 6 to 8 stories from STORIES.md, "interview only" ones included. Map them to the questions that always
come: the hardest problem, something they built or changed, a difficult person, a mistake caught or owned,
work across teams, taking the lead, a tight deadline. Note each story's number and proof.

## Spoken answers, 60 to 90 seconds

- Open with the spoken pitch in PERSONA.md, plus one line on why this job fits.
- Write each mapped story for the ear, 130 to 180 words: the trigger in one sentence, what their own hands
  did (half the answer), the result with its number or scope and its status word, what changed afterwards.
  Credit stays as recorded in the story.
- Run a mock round. Ask one question at a time. Time the answer. Rewrite every line they stumble on. A
  phrase they dislike goes in VOICE.md.

## The careful topics, said aloud

Take the approved wording from "Careful topics" and "Form answers" in SCREEN.md for each one that applies
(a gap, a short stay, a layoff, an unfinished degree, a career change, notice, pay). They practise each in
two or three calm sentences: the true fact, then stop. Nothing is volunteered that wasn't asked.

## A gap in the fit map

For each "gap", prepare the true answer: what they have done that is closest, and how they would learn the
rest. Never a claim to the skill.

## Their questions

Draft four to six plain questions from the posting and from their deal breakers in SCREEN.md and TARGETS.md:
the day-to-day work, the first 90 days, the team, shifts, travel, office days.

## References

Before a final round, read `<data>/REFERENCES.md`. Skip anyone marked "do not call". For a reference not yet
asked, draft the request for the user to send. For each one who agreed, draft a five-line note for the user
to send: the company, the job, the title on the resume, and the two or three stories they may be asked about.

## After each round

Ask, and save in `round-N-review.md`: who they met and what those people cared about, which answers landed
and which questions surprised them, what they learned about the job, the team and the pay, and which answer
needs work before the next round. Offer the thank-you note in `5-cover-letters.md`. Update PIPELINE.md.
