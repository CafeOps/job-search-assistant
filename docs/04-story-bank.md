# 04. Story bank

Stories feed resume bullets (the proof point), interview answers and outreach notes. Collect them in the intake (phase 5). Add one whenever the user mentions a win.

## Format (one per story in `<data>/STORIES.md`)

```
## <short name>
- When / where: <employer, month year>
- Situation: <what was wrong or needed, in one or two lines>
- Their part: <what THEY did, in their words. Not the team's.>
- Result: <what changed, with a number and its status: measured / estimated / identified (not realized)>
- Proof: <ticket, email, doc, dashboard, repo, or "user's word">
- Who else was involved: <names or roles>
- Lanes: <automation / security / platform / customer / leadership / ...>
- Use on resume: yes / interview only / never (and why)
```

## The work log

Mine bullets from a log, not from memory: the dates, numbers and scope are already there. Suggest the user keep one from now on, in `<data>/JOURNAL.md` or wherever they already write. Five lines per notable day is enough:

```
## YYYY-MM-DD
- System: <a tool, a process, a customer>
- What broke or changed: <one line>
- What I did: <one or two lines, their part>
- Number: <count, time, money, scope, or "none">
```

When the log grows, read it for entries with numbers and outcomes and turn the best into stories.

## Drawing out a story

- Ask for the messy version first, then: "What did you do, as opposed to the team?"
- Ask for the number last. Never invent one. Scope works ("every workstation", "all 40 sites").
- Ask: "Could someone at that company dispute this?" If yes, reword it or mark it interview-only.
- Stories that make the employer look bad (an outage, a security gap) stay interview-only unless the user says otherwise.
- Credit follows the work, not the email cc. Ask before splitting credit with a coworker.

## Story to bullet

Pick the lane, lead with the verb, keep one proof point, fit the width band (`docs/03-resume-rules.md`). Shapes: "Built <thing> that <result>, <scope or number>" or "<Verb> <lane> across <scope>, like <one proof point>".
