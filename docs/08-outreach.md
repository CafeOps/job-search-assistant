# 08. Recruiters, agencies and outreach

## Staffing agencies

- Rate first. Before any resume: client name, rate or salary, permanent or contract, length.
- A submitting agency usually holds "right to represent" for that client, which blocks direct applications and better agencies. Never send a resume "to keep on file".
- A recruiter who dodges "perm or contract, what rate?" with "send your resume" is a yellow flag. Ask again. Don't send.

## Cold outreach

- Find the talent advisor or hiring manager on LinkedIn.
- A free LinkedIn connection note caps at about 200 characters, is limited to a few a month and takes no attachment. Say who the user is, the role (req number) and one proof point. Send the resume after they accept.
- An open profile (free "Message" button) allows a longer message with the PDF attached.
- One recruiter per company at a time. Don't ask strangers for referrals.
- Draft only. The user sends each note, or approves its exact text before you send it. Never message anyone from their account on your own.

## Quiet search while employed

- No likes or comments on job-hunt or recruiter posts. Follow quietly. Connect without a note when that's enough.
- The current employer's vendors can talk to the employer. Treat them as visible.
- "Open to work": recruiters-only mode at most, and only if the user agrees.

## Log everything

`<data>/PIPELINE.md`: one row per contact or application (date, company, role and req, contact, what was sent, next step, status), with client names for agencies. Read it before any follow-up. Keep offer letters and other shared documents in `<data>/files/`, never in the kit folder.

## Cover letters

Outreach notes usually do the job. When a letter is needed: one page, three short paragraphs (why this role, two proof points mapped to the posting, a plain close), in the user's voice, checked against the resume's banned list.

## Interviews

One folder per company, `<data>/interviews/<company>/`: the posting, a fit map (posting line to story), company notes, a study sheet, the story entries to use, and a review after each round.
