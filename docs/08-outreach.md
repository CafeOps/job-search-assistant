# 08. Recruiters, agencies and outreach

## Staffing agencies

- Rate first. Before any resume: client name, rate or salary, permanent or contract, length.
- A submitting agency usually holds "right to represent" for that client, which blocks direct applications and better agencies. Never send a resume "to keep on file".
- A recruiter who dodges "perm or contract, what rate?" with "send your resume" is a yellow flag. Ask again. Don't send.

## Cold outreach

- Find the talent advisor or hiring manager on LinkedIn.
- A free LinkedIn connection note caps at about 200 characters, is limited to a few a month and takes no attachment. Say who the user is, the role (req number) and one proof point. Send the resume after they accept.
- An open profile (free "Message" button) allows a longer message with the PDF attached.
- One recruiter per company at a time. Don't ask strangers for referrals.
- Draft only. The user sends each note, or approves its exact text before you send it. Never message anyone from their account on your own.

## Quiet search while employed

- No likes or comments on job-hunt or recruiter posts. Follow quietly. Connect without a note when that's enough.
- The current employer's vendors can talk to the employer. Treat them as visible.
- "Open to work": recruiters-only mode at most, and only if the user agrees. It is not a guarantee: recruiters at related companies, or at agencies the employer uses, may still see it.

## Log everything

`<data>/PIPELINE.md`: one row per contact or application (date, company, role and req, contact, what was sent, next step, status), with client names for agencies. Read it before any follow-up. Keep offer letters and other shared documents in `<data>/files/`, never in the kit folder.

## Follow-ups

- Read `<data>/PIPELINE.md` first. Draft a follow-up only for a row with a named contact or an interview
  under way. The user approves and sends each one.
- An application with a named contact: once after 7 to 10 working days, once more a week later.
- After an interview: 3 working days past the date they gave (5 if they gave none), once more a week later.
- Two unanswered follow-ups: stop. Mark the row "no reply" and move on unless the user reopens it.
- **The 15-application check.** At 15 submitted applications with fewer than 2 recruiter calls, stop and
  look before sending more: how old the postings were when they applied, whether the silent ones match the
  lane and level in TARGETS.md and PERSONA.md, and whether the resume's top third answers those postings.
  Propose ONE change (a tighter lane, a bullet order, more outreach) and record the choice in DECISIONS.md.

## Cover letters

Outreach notes usually do the job. When a letter is needed: one page (why this role, two proof points mapped to the posting, why this employer, a plain close), in the user's voice, checked against the resume's banned list. The full method: `persona/5-cover-letters.md`.

## Interviews

One folder per company, `<data>/interviews/<company>/`: the posting, a fit map (posting line to story), company notes, a study sheet, the story entries to use, and a review after each round. The full method: `persona/7-interview-prep.md`. An offer: `10-offers.md`.
