# Coverage (the stop rule for the deep interview)

Guide: docs/persona/2-deep-interview.md. Updated after every sitting. A cell says "yes" only when the facts
are in the profile files with a source. Story targets: current or main job 5 to 8 (3 to 5 if under two
years), earlier jobs 2 to 4, older or short jobs 1 or 2. Targets, never quotas. Round cap: 12 for the main
job, 6 for any other. Older and short unrelated jobs: mark duties and the dispute check "n/a".
