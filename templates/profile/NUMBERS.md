# Locked numbers

Every figure that can appear on a resume, form or LinkedIn. Status: measured / estimated / identified (not realized).
"Confirmed by user" is "no" until they say yes to that figure, then the date. A figure found in an old
document starts at "no". Only confirmed figures reach a page.
Once the user picks a figure it is locked. Don't re-raise it unless the facts change.
