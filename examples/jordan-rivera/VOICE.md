# Voice

Set by Jordan on 2027-01-16.

## Banned words and phrases (THE one list, paste into every drafting brief)
The kit's default list applies unchanged (templates/profile/VOICE.md).

Punctuation: no em dashes. Avoid semicolons in bullets.

## Added by the user
- Avoid "rockstar", "ninja", "guru" and "wear many hats".
- Do not describe routine report maintenance as a company transformation.
- Do not turn an estimated result into a measured claim.

## Allowed (don't re-flag)
- Built, checked, documented, automated, reconciled, rewrote, SQL, DAX and Power Query.
- "Estimated" beside an estimated result and "identified" beside an unrealized opportunity.

## How they write (from their own samples)
- "The depot totals disagree because the reports use different cutoff dates. I checked the SQL and wrote down which date each report uses."
- "I can own the reporting work. I would want the finance team to confirm the accounting treatment."
- Short sentences. Explain the practical change. Share credit with the people who approved or implemented it.
