Last updated: 2027-02-06. Set by John: pay and benefits 2027-01-14, the rest 2027-01-15 (work authorization 2027-01-13).

## Floors
- Salaried permanent floor: $85,000 CAD annual base (SKIP below). APPLY-IF band: $85,000 to $89,999, and only with a larger internal analytics team, SQL review and written confirmation of the hybrid arrangement. Target: $100,000.
- Contract: allowed. $55 CAD/hr floor as a T4 employee, 12 months minimum, contract-to-perm only: no. It must replace the current job, meet the benefits rules and have a written term and rate before John resigns. No independent-contractor work.
- Non-negotiable benefits: Employer medical coverage by the first day of the month after starting, paid time off and paid holidays. A contract needs these through the agency or client.

## Deal breakers
- Travel over 10%: SKIP.
- On-call / shifts / overtime: No on-call rotation, overnight shifts or recurring weekend work. Occasional planned weekday overtime is fine.
- Security clearance: No roles that require an active clearance or getting one.
- Relocation: No.
- Tech stack or work type they refuse: Pure report queues, sales quotas, primary data engineering ownership, and roles needing production Python engineering on entry.
- Other: Refused titles, more than 2 required office days a week, current-employer contact, unnamed agency submissions. If pay is not posted, check it before applying.

## Form answers (truthful, reusable)
- Legally able to work in: Canada (sponsorship needed: no, now or later).
- Notice period / earliest start: Two weeks after accepting a written offer and clearing required checks. No fixed date promised.
- Non-compete or other-employer commitments: No known non-compete. Confidentiality obligations continue. John leaves the current job before starting a replacement full-time role.
- Degree status wording: Bachelor of Arts in Economics, Toronto Metropolitan University, completed May 2021.
- Salary answer when asked: expectation $100,000 CAD annual base, range $95,000 to $110,000, minimum $85,000. The minimum is a hard floor, not the opening request.
- How they heard about the job: The real recorded source. Default "LinkedIn" only when PIPELINE.md says LinkedIn.
